# ECHOES — Personal Sonic Archive & Listening Telemetry

**ECHOES** is an archival listening intelligence dashboard and interactive audio laboratory. Rather than presenting generic year-end metrics, the platform treats 5.8 years of music history as an audited sonic timeline—translating raw playback logs into tactile thermal receipts, dynamic harmonic waveforms, behavioral anomaly audits, and relational neural maps.

---

## ✦ Core Features & Modules

### 1. Dashboard (Executive Telemetry Hub)
- **Longitudinal Audit Metrics**: Tracks lifetime exposure (1,194 hours across 14,820 streams), circadian shift indices (+42 min/year nocturnal drift), and emotional dispersion ratios.
- **Chronobiological Rhythm Matrix**: 24-hour day-part breakdown correlating peak listening windows (Nocturnal, Dawn, Twilight, Midday) with dominant genres and acoustic moods.
- **Atmospheric & Mood Radar**: Multi-axis acoustic parameter analysis covering valence, acousticness, energy, danceability, and dynamic range.
- **Obsession Loop Audits**: Quantifies consecutive single-track repeat streaks (such as 32 consecutive days of *Kyoto* by Phoebe Bridgers).

### 2. Temporal Sonic Stream (Dynamic Chronicle Waveform)
- **Multi-Era Year Scrubber (2019–2024 & All-Time)**: Generates dynamic Bezier spline curves calculating monthly listening amplitude, tempo fluctuations (BPM), and obsession spikes.
- **Seasonal Solstice Lenses**: Filters playback profiles across Winter, Spring, Summer, and Autumn with dedicated tempo metrics and mood profiles.
- **Three Specialized View Modes**:
  - **Chronicle Waveform**: Smooth harmonic curves with interactive monthly inspection nodes and obsession badges.
  - **Season Matrix**: 4-solstice comparative breakdown of acoustic density and dominant anchors.
  - **Tape Reel**: Analog cassette spool interface with calibrated tape speeds, magnetic reels, and continuous playback counters.

### 3. Catalog & Thermal Ledger (Itemized Track & Artist Audit)
- **Audited Library & Search**: Search and filter capabilities across artists, genres, eras, and acoustic tags.
- **Dual-Mode Audio Engine**: Plays 30-second audio stream previews alongside an interactive Web Audio API synthesizer.
- **Live Query & Metadata Lookup**: Quick Spotify search integration with live query previews and audio feature metrics.

### 4. Neural Tapestry (Connection Explorer)
- **Multi-Node Relational Graph**: Maps connections between artists, landmark tracks, listening habits, and emotional eras.
- **Three Dynamic Layout Engines**:
  - **Constellation**: Organic network layout highlighting clustered musical affinities.
  - **Era Rings**: Three concentric orbital rings organizing Era Cores, Artist Rings, and Signature Tracks.
  - **Chord Flow**: Circular perimeter distribution connected via quadratic Bezier paths.

### 5. Behavioral Patterns (Habit & Anomaly Audits)
- **Psychological Listening Audits**: Analyzes listening quirks such as the *Pre-Dawn Sadcore Loop*, *Sunday Dusk Melancholia*, *Hyper-Focus Velocity*, and *Solstice Timber Pivot*.
- **Pattern Catalysts**: Direct triggers to stream catalyst tracks associated with specific psychological states.

### 6. Archival Thermal Receipts & Export System
- **Tactile Paper Receipt Modal**: Renders vintage, perforated-edge thermal paper receipts with itemized audit numbers, barcodes, timestamps, and acoustic metadata.
- **Production Print Styling**: Dedicated `@media print` stylesheet that isolates the receipt on clean white paper and strips away web navigation for physical printing or PDF export.

## ✦ Design Identity & Aesthetics

- **Atmosphere**: Technical twilight palette (`#11131b` deep charcoal, `#191b24` card surfaces, `#d0bcff` violet accents, `#ffb95f` amber highlights).
- **Typography Pairing**: Syne for bold display titles, Inter for legible UI text, and JetBrains Mono for telemetry metrics, timestamps, and receipt printouts.
- **Accessibility & Shortcuts**: Global `Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K` keyboard shortcut for the quick search palette, full keyboard navigation, and responsive touch targets.

---

## ✦ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React 18+ with TypeScript & Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom fonts and print styles
- **Icons**: Google Material Symbols Outlined & Lucide React
- **Audio Engine**: HTML5 Audio & Web Audio API (real-time sine-wave synthesizer)
